## 📌 Executive Summary
This project delivers a comprehensive, end-to-end data analytics workflow designed to investigate urban traffic accident patterns across major metropolitan centers in India. By leveraging **Microsoft Excel** for raw data cleaning, **SQL** for relational transformation & metric aggregation, and **Power BI** for advanced visual storytelling, raw crash data was converted into an executive-ready analytical platform.

The goal is to provide actionable intelligence on spatial accident clusters, temporal risk triggers, light/weather dependencies, and primary causal variables to drive data-backed road safety interventions.

---

## 🎯 Key Analytical Highlights
- **Spatial Risk Identification:** Pinpointed high-density accident corridors and geographic hotspots across states and urban zones.
- **Temporal & Environmental Analysis:** Correlated crash frequencies with specific hours, ambient lighting conditions, and severe weather states.
- **Root-Cause Drill-Down:** Evaluated driver behavioral factors, road classification impacts, and vehicle type involvements using AI-driven decomposition models.
