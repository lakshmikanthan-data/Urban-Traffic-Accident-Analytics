## 📌 Executive Summary
This project delivers an end-to-end data analytics solution examining urban traffic accidents across major Indian cities. By combining **Excel**, **SQL**, and **Power BI**, raw incident logs were transformed into an interactive 4-page executive report. The analytical focus centers on spatial risk clusters, environmental impact metrics, temporal trends, and causal factor breakdowns to support data-driven urban safety strategies.

---

## 🛠️ End-to-End Workflow

### 1. Data Cleaning & Preparation (Microsoft Excel)
- Standardized data types, missing value imputation, and timestamp field formatting.
- Structured initial categorical variables (Vehicle Types, Weather Conditions, Severity Groups).

### 2. Analytical Data Aggregation (SQL)
- Wrote aggregation queries to extract key metrics like **Total Incident Counts**, **Fatality Rate %**, and **Casualty Ratios**.
- Segmented data across multiple dimensions (Geographical Hotspots, Time Intervals, and Driver Behavior).

### 3. Visual Analytics & Reporting (Power BI Desktop)
- Designed a custom 4-page dark-themed layout (*Slate / City Park* palette) optimized for visual contrast and executive reporting.
- Configured dynamic button-based navigation and visual-level slicer cross-filtering.
- Implemented AI-driven **Decomposition Trees** for root-cause drill-down analysis.

## 📊 Dashboard Architecture & Key Pages

- **Page 1: Executive Overview** — High-level safety metrics, overall incident volumes, fatality percentage, and baseline severity trends.
- **Page 2: Spatial Hotspots Analysis** — Spatial distributions across states/cities highlighting high-risk traffic zones.
- **Page 3: Temporal & Weather Analytics** — Time-of-day crash patterns cross-analyzed with light conditions and weather factors.
- **Page 4: Causal Factors & Vehicle Breakdown** — Detailed analysis of driver behavior, road categories, vehicle types, and root-cause decomposition.
